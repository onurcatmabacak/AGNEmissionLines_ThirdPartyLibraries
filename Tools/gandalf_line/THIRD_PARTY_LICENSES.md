## BC03/Tremonti04 templates

`third_party/templates/BC03_Tremonti04/`,
`third_party/templates/BC03_Tremonti04_templates.dat`

The stellar-population templates distributed with the GANDALF v1.5 SDSS
example.  They are based on Bruzual & Charlot (2003) models and the
Tremonti et al. (2004) SDSS library.  Cite those papers and the GANDALF paper
when publishing results.
