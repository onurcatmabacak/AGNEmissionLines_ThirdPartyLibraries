## Tests and caveats

| test | data | method | interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| PyQSOFit UV/optical vs SDSS ZLINE | Mg II, C III], C IV, Hβ, [O III] for five QSOs | one-sample t-test and Wilcoxon signed-rank on log(total/ZLINE); negative-control narrow components | Small n; C III] broad is blend-limited and is not a narrow-line measurement. Hβ and Mg II are the more meaningful comparisons. |
| CIGALE vs AGNfitter | five QSOs, best CIGALE family by χ²_ν | paired Wilcoxon/t-test on log L_AGN, log M*, AGN fraction; Spearman correlation | Model-family scatter is the dominant systematic; no single-family claim. |
| FIR recovery | four synthetic lines | recovered/truth median and bootstrap 16–84 % CI | median 0.983 and CI 0.941–1.064; OI/OIII/CII near truth, NII 347±67 vs 300. |
| Cross-band consistency | 13 real+synthetic ratios | fraction within broad priors; binomial test vs 0.5 | The 0.5 null is only a reference, not a physical model; priors are broad. |
| PAHFIT stability | thirteen segments/fits | rms_rel + quality flags | 11/13 numerically stable; **all production fits are flagged/systematics-limited**. PDS short/long/both 0.0088/0.017/0.038; NGC 4151 0.031; VV114 0.195 (hot-dust mismatch); NGC 253 0.25 (gaps). Legacy NGC 253 tails remain unstable at 0.71/11.5. |
| Same-object X-ray/optical | RXTE 3C 120 + XMM PDS 456 | per-object luminosity/EW, quality flags | 3C 120 H-beta provisional (model range 1.55–3.00); PDS 456 local H-beta provisional (χ²_red 430); no slope fit at N=2 |
| Cross-tool line ratios | SDSS ZLINE + QSOFITMORE, PyQSOFit, PyQSOFitSpec, S3Fit, GANDALF, GalSpec, QGfit, SHEAP, PAHFIT | per-source median, 1000-sample bootstrap CI, log scatter | 04592649654 Hβ/[O III] 5007: ZLINE **1.57**, fitters **1.83–2.50**; nine-entry median **2.04**, 0.053 dex. PyQSOFit/S3Fit/GANDALF alone agree at 0.05 dex. |

The comparison figure is **not** a replacement for the per-object fit figures;

## MaNGA DAP subset validation

The official MaNGA DAP branch (`manga_dap`) is validated separately: the
full-method SASUKE fit on the 9 central single-spaxel bins of NGC 1194
(9193-12701) is compared at the same spaxels to the published DAP MAPS.  The
median Hα/Hβ/[O III]/[N II]/[S II] ratios are **0.9995-0.99999**, BPT agrees
**9/9**, and the Hα/stellar kinematic offsets are <0.02 km/s.  This is a
**subset** validation; the all-spaxel 2397-bin DAP emission-line model
exceeded the 58 min budget and was killed, so the published MAPS is the
all-spaxel reference.  The fast fixed-continuum all-spaxel mode is rejected as
production (`FASTMODE_NOT_PRODUCTION`).  See
`outputs/NGC1194_z_0.0136/manga_dap/comparison/`.
