Outputs `ln Z`, Bayes factors and 16/50/84 posteriors. Two solver findings are recorded: UltraNest's default `speed="safe"` never switches to slice sampling, which stalled the 5-parameter partial-covering model at ~2 % efficiency for ten hours (`speed="auto"` raises it to 25-42 %); and a broad Fe K-alpha with a boundary-pinned sigma (0.79 keV against a 0.8 keV prior cap, centroid 7.0 keV rest) is degenerate with continuum curvature, so its Bayes factor is not a line detection. Nested sampling then integrates an essentially flat prior (live points start at log-likelihood −9e6 and never close their band), which is why the run burned 428k evaluations without converging. Dropping the model is the correct move; a Compton-thick comparison needs a broadband (NuSTAR) dataset. Every model also sits at χ²/dof ≈ 2.3-2.7, so an unmodelled component (soft excess / warm absorption / cross-calibration) must be fixed before any Bayes factor is quoted. - 3C 120 PyQSOFit 1.55 vs QSOFITMORE 3.00: a real model-systematic spread; crossmission is provisional.
- PAHFIT `flagged=true`: a scientific quality flag, not a runner failure.
